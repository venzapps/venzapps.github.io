Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

VenzApps ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Textly - TXT Editor mobile application ("Textly" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how Textly handles information when you use the App.

Textly is designed primarily as an offline text editor. The core text editing features work locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

VenzApps does not directly collect, store, or sell your personal information through Textly's core offline features.

Textly does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, home address, or other personal details to use the core features of the App.

1.1 Text Files and Documents

Textly allows you to create, open, edit, save, share, print, and export text documents.

Your text files and their contents are processed locally on your device.

VenzApps does not upload your text documents or their contents to VenzApps servers.

Textly does not provide cloud storage or cloud synchronization for your text files.

When you choose to open or save a file, Android may provide access to the file or storage location that you selected.

2. Offline Operation

Textly is designed to provide its core text editing functionality offline.

You can create, view, edit, and manage supported text files locally on your device without creating a VenzApps account or uploading your documents to our servers.

Some features, such as advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads, require an internet connection.

3. Advertising

Textly contains advertisements provided through Google Mobile Ads.

Google Mobile Ads is a third-party advertising service. Although Textly does not collect your personal information for its own servers, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information to provide advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention services.

Depending on the configuration and services used, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising ID, where available
  • App and device identifiers
  • App interactions
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • Information used for fraud prevention

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

You can also learn more about Google's advertising technologies at:

Google Advertising Technologies

4. Your Text Files

Textly is a local text editor. Your text documents are handled on your device when you use the App's editing and file-management features.

VenzApps does not receive or store the contents of your documents on its servers.

Textly does not intentionally send the contents of your text documents to Google or advertising providers.

If you use Android's sharing functionality, the file may be transferred to another app or service according to your selection. Such transfers are initiated by you and are controlled by the destination app or service.

5. File Access and Permissions

Textly may access files when you choose to create, open, save, share, delete, or otherwise manage files.

Android's file picker and document provider mechanisms may be used to allow you to select files or storage locations.

Textly only uses file access for functionality provided by the App.

Textly does not use your text files for advertising.

6. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Textly may store certain information locally on your device to provide application functionality.

This may include:

  • Application preferences
  • Theme settings
  • Font settings
  • Editor settings
  • Recent file information
  • Pinned file information
  • Draft or recovery information

This locally stored information is not uploaded to VenzApps servers.

7. Data Sharing

VenzApps does not sell your personal information.

VenzApps does not sell, rent, or trade your text documents or their contents.

Textly does not intentionally share your document contents with advertisers.

Third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads may process certain device, advertising, interaction, and diagnostic information as necessary to provide their services.

8. Third-Party Services

Textly may use third-party services to provide advertisements and related services.

Google Mobile Ads

Textly uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google's data practices are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Textly is designed to keep your text documents locally on your device rather than uploading them to VenzApps servers.

Third-party services used by the App are responsible for securing the information they process according to their respective security practices.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

VenzApps does not maintain a cloud database containing your text documents.

Text files remain in the storage location chosen by you unless you delete or move them.

Local Textly application data can generally be removed by clearing the App's data through Android settings or by uninstalling Textly.

Files stored outside Textly's private application storage may remain on your device after uninstalling the App and may need to be deleted separately.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Textly is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

VenzApps does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Textly.
