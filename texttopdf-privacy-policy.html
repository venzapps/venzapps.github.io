Your documents stay under your control

Text to PDF creates, edits and converts documents on your device. We do not intentionally upload your document content to a VenzApps server for editing or PDF conversion.

1. Overview

Text to PDF is an Android app by VenzApps for creating, editing, importing and converting documents to PDF. This policy explains how your files are handled and what technical information may be processed by services used in the app.

You do not need an account to use the app's core document features.

2. Documents & local storage

Documents you create or import, including text, formatting, tables and embedded images, are processed locally for editing and PDF generation. The app may store documents and preferences on your device.

You choose which files to open, import, export or share. If you share a PDF through another app or save it to a location managed by another provider, that destination handles the file under its own policies.

We do not intentionally include document text, document titles, filenames, image contents or file paths in custom analytics events.

3. Information collected

Although document processing is local, the app uses third-party services for analytics, diagnostics and advertisements. Depending on your device, settings and applicable consent choices, these services may process:

  • App activity: app opens, feature interactions and operation outcomes.
  • Device and app details: device model, Android version, app version and technical diagnostics.
  • Identifiers: app-instance, installation or advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Approximate location: an estimated region derived from network information such as IP address; the app does not need GPS for these services.

The sections below explain each service. We do not require your name, email address or account login for normal document editing.

4. Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how Text to PDF is used and decide what to improve. Analytics may record app opens, sessions, app updates and interactions such as document creation, import results, PDF preview or export outcomes, and use of selected app features.

Our custom events are intended to contain only predefined technical categories and counts, not what you write or the contents of files. Google Analytics also automatically processes information such as app-instance identifiers, device details and approximate geography, subject to its configuration and applicable consent settings.

Learn more in Google's Firebase Analytics data collection information.

5. Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes, unexpected errors and app stability problems. Crash reports may contain exception types, stack traces, diagnostic details, app and device versions, timestamps, installation identifiers and related app activity when available.

We aim to avoid adding document contents, filenames or local file paths to custom error reports. Technical exceptions may still contain information generated by Android or software libraries, so crash reporting is reviewed and minimized where possible; it is not described as completely anonymous or perfectly redacted.

Learn more from Firebase privacy information.

6. Google AdMob & advertising choices

Text to PDF uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The app may also offer optional rewarded ads that provide temporary ad-free time after a reward is earned.

Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, advertising or other device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, depending on applicable settings and consent.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may display an advertising consent message. Privacy choices may be available within the app when required, and relevant advertising settings may also be managed through Android or Google controls. Available choices depend on location and configuration.

See How Google uses information for advertising.

7. File access & permissions

The app accesses documents and destinations you select through Android file pickers or other supported file-access methods. Network connectivity may be used by advertising, analytics and crash-reporting services; normal document conversion does not require uploading your document to us.

You can review app permissions in Android Settings. Any additional permission used by a specific feature should be explained when requested.

8. Third-party services

The app uses Google services, including Google Play Services, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Google UMP may be used to present required advertising privacy messages.

These services handle data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. For details, visit Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

9. Data retention & deletion

Documents saved inside the app generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to Android storage and backup behavior. Files exported to other locations may remain there until deleted separately.

Analytics, crash and advertising data may be retained by the relevant service providers according to their settings and retention policies. Deleting a local document or uninstalling the app does not automatically remove information previously processed by these providers.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the email address below. Some requests may be limited where we cannot identify a particular diagnostic or analytics record.

10. Your choices

  • Open, manage or delete locally stored documents using available app controls.
  • Choose which files and locations to access through Android.
  • Review app permissions and relevant advertising settings on your device.
  • Review or change advertising consent through the app's privacy options when that feature is available or required.
  • Contact us with questions about analytics, crash reports or privacy.

11. Security

Core document processing is designed to occur locally. Google SDK communications generally use encrypted connections in transit. We take reasonable steps to limit custom diagnostic data, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Children's privacy

Text to PDF does not require an account or intentionally request personal details from children for its document-editing features. If you have concerns about data processed in connection with a child, contact us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, service providers, privacy practices or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

14. Contact us

If you have questions, feedback or privacy-related requests about Text to PDF, contact VenzApps.
