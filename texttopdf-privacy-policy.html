Privacy Policy for Text to PDF

Text to PDF is designed to help you create, edit, manage, and convert text documents into professional PDF files while keeping your document workflow simple.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Text to PDF ("Text to PDF", "we", "our", or "us").

Text to PDF is an Android application that allows users to create and edit text documents, format content, manage documents, preview PDF output, and convert documents into PDF files.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access, how information is used, and how your information is handled.

🔒 Privacy-focused document workflow

Your documents are primarily handled on your device. Text to PDF does not require you to upload your document content to a server in order to create or convert a PDF.

2. Information We Collect

Text to PDF is designed to minimize the information required to provide its core document creation and PDF conversion features.

📄

Document Content

Text and document content that you create, edit, or open in the application may be processed on your device to provide the editing and PDF conversion features.

📁

Files

The application may access files that you choose to open, import, save, or export through the Android file system.

⚙️

App Settings

Application preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device to remember your selected options.

📊

Advertising Information

If advertisements are displayed, advertising providers may collect information as described in their applicable privacy policies and settings.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Your passwords or authentication credentials.
  • The contents of unrelated personal files.
  • Your messages or emails.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your web browsing history.

3. Documents & Files

Text to PDF allows you to create, edit, open, save, and convert documents.

📄 Your documents stay under your control

Documents that you create or open are processed by the application to provide editing, formatting, preview, and PDF generation functionality.

When you choose to open or save a file using Android's file picker or storage system, the application may receive access to the specific file or location that you select.

Text to PDF does not intentionally upload your document content to a Text to PDF-operated remote server for normal document editing or PDF generation.

4. Permissions

Text to PDF requests Android permissions only when they are required to provide an application feature.

File Access

File access may be required when you choose to open, import, save, or export documents through Android's file system.

Notifications

If notification functionality is implemented and enabled, notification permission may be used to provide relevant application notifications.

Other Permissions

The application may request additional permissions if required for specific features introduced in future versions.

You can manage application permissions through Android system settings.

5. Local Storage

Text to PDF may store application data and document information locally on your device.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Documents created within the application.
  • Document names and metadata.
  • Application preferences.
  • Editor settings.
  • Other information required for the application's normal operation.
🔐
Local-first document processing

Core document editing and PDF generation functionality is designed to operate locally on your Android device.

6. Advertising

Text to PDF may display advertisements to support the continued development and availability of the application.

The application may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, diagnostics, and interactions with advertisements, depending on the applicable configuration, consent choices, and regional requirements.

📢 Advertising privacy

Advertising-related information is processed by the applicable advertising provider under its own privacy policy, terms, and applicable consent requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Text to PDF may rely on third-party services to provide certain application functionality, advertising, distribution, analytics, or supporting services.

These services may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the applicable third-party providers for additional information about their data practices.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Text to PDF.

The application is designed so that its core document editing and PDF generation operations can be performed locally on your device.

🛡️
Device-based document processing

Normal document creation, editing, formatting, and PDF generation do not require sending your document content to a Text to PDF remote server.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Documents and application information stored locally generally remain on your device until you delete or modify them, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

You can manage your documents through the application's available document management features.

Uninstalling Text to PDF generally removes the application's local data according to Android's standard application behavior.

Information processed by third-party services, including advertising providers, may be retained according to those providers' respective policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Text to PDF does not intentionally collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us using the email address provided below.

11. Your Choices

You have control over several aspects of Text to PDF and the information handled by the application.

  • Create, edit, save, and delete your documents.
  • Choose which files the application can access through Android's file picker.
  • Manage application permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage notifications through Android settings, where applicable.
  • Delete application data through Android settings.
  • Uninstall Text to PDF at any time.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Text to PDF, its features, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated.

We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information about our privacy practices.
