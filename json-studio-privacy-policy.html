Privacy Policy – JSON Studio

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the JSON Studio - View Edit Export Android application developed by VenzApps.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information JSON Studio handles, how it is used, and how your data is protected when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

JSON Studio does not require you to create an account or sign in. The application does not directly collect personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or contact details.

JSON files you open, create, edit, or process in JSON Studio are processed locally on your Android device. Your JSON files are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Local App Data

JSON Studio may store certain information locally on your device to provide its features.

This may include:

  • Names of recently opened files
  • File URIs required to access recently used files
  • Pasted JSON content stored in the application's private storage until cleared

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to VenzApps servers.

3. JSON Files and Content

JSON Studio is designed to process JSON files directly on your device.

Your JSON files and their contents are not sent to VenzApps servers or stored in cloud storage operated by VenzApps.

You are responsible for the content of the files you open or create using the application.

4. Advertising

JSON Studio uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use certain information for advertising purposes. This may include information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Usage information
  • IP address

The information collected by Google may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Third-Party Services

JSON Studio uses Google AdMob for advertising. We do not use other third-party analytics, tracking services, or cloud storage services within the application.

6. Data Security

JSON Studio processes JSON files locally on your Android device. VenzApps does not receive or store your JSON file contents on its own servers.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your Android device and operating system.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data remains on your device until it is removed through the available application features or Android system settings.

You can clear application data or uninstall JSON Studio through your Android device settings to remove locally stored application data.

8. Your Choices

You can choose which JSON files to open or create using the application.

You can also remove locally stored application data through the application's available settings or through Android system settings.

9. Children's Privacy

JSON Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can address the concern.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
